🚀 Crack AI – AI Powered Competitive Exam Preparation Platform

Crack AI is a fully AI-powered EdTech platform designed to help students prepare smarter for competitive exams like JEE, NEET, UPSC, GATE, CAT, and more.

This platform combines Artificial Intelligence, personalized learning, analytics, and smart study tools to provide an advanced exam preparation experience for students.
